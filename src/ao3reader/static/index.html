AO3 Reader

Paste a link to a work and it's read aloud to you. Each chapter is ready to play as soon as it's read.

Voice and options

Open it in your browser (logged in), click Entire Work, save the page, then choose the saved file here and press Read it.

How it works

  1. Paste a link to a work on AO3 and press Read it.
  2. The first part is ready in a minute or two. Press play and listen while the rest is read.
  3. When it's all done, download the audiobook to keep it on your phone.

Listen on your phone

Point your phone's camera at this code. Your phone needs to be on the same Wi-Fi, and this computer needs to stay on while it reads.

Your audiobooks

    To keep one on your phone, download it, then open it with Books or BookPlayer on an iPhone, or an audiobook app such as Voice on Android.
