    Getting a more natural voice

    Your iPhone has better voices you can download for free. Go to Settings, Accessibility, Spoken Content (called Read & Speak on newer iPhones), Voices, English, and download one marked Enhanced or Premium. Then pick it under “Voice and settings” while a story is open.

    Your iPhone stops a web page from talking when the screen locks, so this reader keeps the screen on while it reads.
