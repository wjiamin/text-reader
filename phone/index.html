    Getting a more natural voice

    Your iPhone has better voices you can download for free. Go to Settings, Accessibility, Spoken Content (called Read & Speak on newer iPhones), Voices, English, and download one marked Enhanced or Premium. Then pick it under “Voice and settings” while a story is open.

    Your iPhone stops a web page from talking when the screen locks, so this reader keeps the screen on while it reads.

    Voice and settings

    The natural voice is made on this phone, so it downloads once and then works offline. Prepared parts play with the screen locked; making new ones needs this page open. If your phone makes audio slower than you listen, prepare the rest of the story while it charges.

    A much more natural voice runs in the GitHub version of Fic Listener, which can download it: wjiamin.github.io/text-reader

    For a more natural voice, download an Enhanced or Premium voice in your iPhone's settings (see the tip on the stories page).

    Downloaded a voice and don't see it? Close Safari completely (swipe it away in the app switcher), open it again, and come back here. Use Safari itself, not a page inside another app.
